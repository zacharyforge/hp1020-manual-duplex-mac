# HP1020 Manual Duplex 4

## English

- Added **All pages** and **Page range** modes, including even-numbered starts, blank first/last endpoints, and bilingual validation.
- Choosing or opening a PDF only selects it. Printing starts only after **Start Printing** is clicked.
- The selected filename remains visible while binding direction and page range are reviewed.
- Successful printing ends quietly with a status-line message; error alerts and the required in-window paper-reload step remain.
- The upper-right language label now always reads **语言 / Language**.
- Added a simple app icon made from two overlapping rounded rectangles.
- Included the editable SVG source, generated ICNS file, and reproducible icon-generation scripts.
- The app icon includes modern PNG-backed ICNS sizes from 16 through 1024 pixels.

## 中文

- 新增“全部页”和“指定页码”，支持从偶数页开始、起止页留空以及双语页码校验。
- 选择或打开 PDF 时只会选中文件；只有点击“开始打印”才会提交任务。
- 确认装订方向和页码范围时，窗口会持续显示所选文件名。
- 打印成功后只显示状态文字；错误弹窗和必要的窗口内重新放纸步骤保持不变。
- 右上角语言标签现在始终显示为“语言 / Language”。
- 新增由两个重叠圆角矩形组成的简洁 App 图标。
- 包含可编辑的 SVG 源文件、生成好的 ICNS 文件以及可复现的图标生成脚本。
- App 图标的现代 PNG 型 ICNS 资源覆盖 16 至 1024 像素尺寸。
